DISCOVER THE ISLAND

The Island of Peace

Jeju Island is a stunning volcanic paradise located just off the southern coast of South Korea. It is known for its natural wonders, Mount Hallasan, and fascinating lava tube caves.

🌋

Location

Jeju is located in southern South Korea and is a volcanic island.

🌤️

Climate

Jeju has a mild oceanic climate, making it pleasant to visit during much of the year.

⛰️

Mount Hallasan

It is the highest mountain in South Korea and one of Jeju's most important natural landmarks.

CULTURE AND TRADITIONS

A Unique Way of Life

01

Haenyeo

They are women free-divers who dive into the ocean without oxygen tanks to collect seafood.

02

Dol Harubang

They are grandfather-shaped statues carved from volcanic rock and are traditional symbols of the island.

03

Tradition

Jeju culture is deeply connected to the sea, nature, and the lives of its people.

THE PERFECT ESCAPE

Why Visit Jeju?

Jeju offers the perfect balance between adventure and relaxation. It is an ideal place to escape busy city life, breathe fresh air, and connect with nature.

FAMOUS LOCALS

Seungkwan and Soyou

Jeju is the hometown of well-known artists such as Seungkwan, a member of SEVENTEEN, and singer Soyou. Both are known for sharing their connection and love for the island.

✦

Fun Fact

The K-drama When Life Gives You Tangerines showcases the beauty and history of Jeju.
